# Data center facility operations

The chapter will give the facility background that the simulated data hall and the fault types are based on.

## Power and cooling infrastructure

The power chain (utility, generators, UPS, PDU) and the cooling chain (chiller, pumps, CRAH units) will be described, together with N+1 redundancy and the quantities that are typically measured.

## Thermal limits and dense racks

The ASHRAE thermal guidelines and the 27 °C inlet limit will be presented, and the effect of dense GPU racks on how fast a cooling failure becomes critical will be explained.

## Outages and their causes

Recent industry outage studies will be summarised to show the frequency, cost and causes of outages, with a focus on power and cooling failures.

## Monitoring and manual response

The section will describe how BMS and DCIM systems monitor facilities today, and why alarm handling by people is slow and depends on staffing.
