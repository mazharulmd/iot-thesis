# Results

The chapter will report the results of the evaluations without interpreting them.

## Detector evaluation

The detectors will be compared on simulated test data by detection rate, F1, time to detect and false alarms.

## Validation on real equipment data

The detectors will be evaluated on SKAB and compared with static limits and the best published results.

## Fault response (E1) and sensitivity

The time to recover of the three modes will be compared for every fault type, and the effect of the assumed human response time will be analysed.

## Robustness (E4) and fault severity (E5)

The results will be reported under load surges, faulty sensors, message loss and three severity levels.

## Cloud pipeline: latency, scalability and cost

The end-to-end check, the load test at 50, 500 and 2,000 gateways, the cost model and the measured AWS bill will be reported.

## Summary of the hypotheses

A table will show whether each hypothesis H1–H8 passed.
